<p><strong>Nutanix AHV</strong> has emerged as a clear alternative, simple to manage, cost-efficient, cloud-ready, and expanding fast—<strong>adding over <a href="https://ir.nutanix.com/news-releases/news-release-details/nutanix-reports-fourth-quarter-and-fiscal-2025-financial-results">2,700 customers</a> in their 4th quarter</strong>.</p>



<p>Now, for those moving to AHV, there is an <strong>even bigger advantage</strong> as <strong>NetScaler VPX</strong> is fully integrated, fully supported, and available right from Nutanix Prism.</p>



<p>Spin it up in minutes, and instantly layer enterprise-grade load balancing, security, and app delivery intelligence across your entire Nutanix stack.</p>

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Why NetScaler is in a Class of Its Own</h2>



<p>Plenty of load balancers can move traffic. NetScaler transforms how traffic is secured, optimized, and delivered at an astonishing speed due to NetScaler’s <strong>One-Pass Architecture</strong>, where the traffic is processed, secured, and accelerated in a single flow. It has cut latency by up to 89% compared to F5 BIG-IP VE (<a href="https://www.netscaler.com/blog/application-delivery/netscaler-outperforms-f5-in-third-party-validated-testing-by-the-tolly-group/">per independent testing</a>).</p>



<p>NetScaler had never paused on needed innovation that would bring real benefit to our customers. The following are few examples:</p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Linux-Based platform Debut on AHV</strong> – First release of NetScaler’s modern Linux architecture, delivering superior platform security, faster boot times, and better performance.</li>



<li><strong>Zero-Touch Certificate Management</strong> – Automatically discovers, renews, and applies TLS/SSL certs—no downtime, no manual steps.</li>



<li><strong>Hybrid Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) </strong>– Combines X25519 with ML-KEM768 to protect against the “harvest-now, decrypt-later” quantum threat.</li>



<li><strong>Next-Gen Kubernetes Ingress (Gateway API)</strong> – Standardizes the way you manage app traffic across clusters and clouds to reduce operational toil, expedite safe releases, and deliver consistent, portable policy control.</li>
</ul>

<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Deep Synergies Inside the Nutanix Ecosystem</h1>



<p>This isn’t just about AHV hypervisor compatibility, it’s about native integration across the Nutanix stack. </p>



<ul class="wp-block-list">
<li><strong>Secure Gateway for VDI</strong> – High-performance, secure access for Citrix Virtual Apps and Desktops hosted on Nutanix infrastructure.</li>



<li><strong>Ingress for Nutanix Kubernetes Service (NKS</strong>) – Enterprise-grade north-south and east-west traffic management for Karbon/NKS clusters, with TLS termination, Layer 7 routing, and WAF protection for containerized apps.</li>



<li><strong>Enterprise ADC for AHV Workloads</strong> – Intelligent load balancing for business-critical apps, delivering fast failover and optimal performance.</li>



<li><strong>Application Security at Scale</strong> – Advanced WAF, bot protection, API security, and zero-trust access—right where your workloads run.</li>
</ul>



<p>With NetScaler on Nutanix, every part of the stack—<strong>from VDI to Kubernetes—is protected, optimized, and future-ready.</strong></p>
