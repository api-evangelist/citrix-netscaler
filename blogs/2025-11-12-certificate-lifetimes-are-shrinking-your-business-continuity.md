<p>Certificate management has quietly become one of the most urgent operational challenges in enterprise IT. As the industry moves from multi-year SSL/TLS certificates to validity periods as short as <strong>47 days</strong>, organizations will struggle to keep pace. While this move strengthens the internet’s security posture, it also poses a serious challenge for enterprises: keeping up with this pace manually will result in an operational burden that exposes businesses to outages, compliance gaps, and reputational risk.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p>



<p>For many organizations, certificate management still relies on spreadsheets, calendar reminders, and manual coordination between IT, security, and application teams. Every certificate must be requested, approved, installed, and bound to services—then monitored for expiration. As certificate lifetimes shrink, those <strong>manual workflows won&#8217;t scale</strong> in the world of microservices, APIs, and hybrid deployments. It only takes one missed renewal to cause a public outage, fail an audit, or damage customer trust.&nbsp;</p>



<p>The business risk is real. A single expired certificate can take down a critical customer-facing application or disrupt secure access for employees. In high-traffic or regulated environments, that downtime can translate into millions in lost revenue and reputational harm. Even when renewals happen on time, the manual workload drains valuable IT hours and shifts attention away from innovation. Security and network teams spend their days chasing expiration dates instead of focusing on business-critical projects.&nbsp;</p>



<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Automation Is the Only Sustainable Path Forward&nbsp;</h2>



<p>NetScaler solves this growing problem with its <strong>Zero-Touch Certificate Management (ZTCM)</strong> solution and <strong>ACME protocol integration</strong>, both currently available via NetScaler Console, bringing true end-to-end automation to SSL/TLS lifecycle management. Once configured, certificates issue, renew, and deploy automatically—without human intervention. NetScaler Console will manage the entire certificate lifecycle, including issuance, renewal, and automated deployment, whether on-premises or in the cloud.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Through ACME—the open standard trusted by leading certificate authorities—organizations can integrate directly with leading CAs, DigiCert and Let’s Encrypt. This eliminates proprietary workflows and vendor lock-in, enabling secure, standards-based certificate automation that works seamlessly across hybrid and multi-cloud environments.&nbsp;</p>



<p>NetScaler Zero Touch Certificate Management also provides centralized visibility and policy control, giving IT leaders a single dashboard to monitor certificate health, enforce governance, and generate audit-ready reports. Compliance becomes proactive instead of reactive, and operational risk drops dramatically.&nbsp;</p>
