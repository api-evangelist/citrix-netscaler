<p>NetScaler MPX 9100 FIPS and 8900 FIPS release version 13.1, has achieved certification to be on the<a href="https://aplits.disa.mil/processAPList" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank"> Department of Defense Information Network Approved Products List (DoDIN APL)</a>.</p>



<p>The DoDIN APL is a U.S. military compliance framework. The purpose of the Approved Products List is to maintain a consolidated list of products that have passed both Interoperability and Cybersecurity certifications. These rigorous tests are used to certify products that affect communication and collaboration across the DoDIN and provide acquisitional guidance for DoD organizations looking to use those products.</p>



<p>This certification serves as a major milestone for NetScaler and demonstrates our commitment to providing secure and interoperable products for DoD organizations.&nbsp;</p>



<p>As defense organizations operate in increasingly distributed environments, securing access to sensitive data beyond traditional network boundaries is more critical than ever. NetScaler addresses this challenge with a software-based architecture and unified codebase across all deployment types—ensuring consistent security policies and streamlined management across data center, cloud, and edge deployments. This unified approach simplifies operations, strengthens security, and ensures mission-critical applications remain available and responsive wherever they run.</p>



<p>NetScaler helps to enhance availability and protect cybersecurity and interoperability through a range of capabilities. Key features include advanced load balancing (LB and GSLB), web application firewall (WAF), API and bot protection, SSL offloading, DoS mitigation, and Zero Trust access enforcement.&nbsp;</p>



<p>Learn more about the Approved Products List and find the NetScaler certified listing on the <a href="https://aplits.disa.mil/processAPList" rel="noreferrer noopener" target="_blank">DoDIN Website</a> (use the Keyword Search for “NetScaler”)</p>
